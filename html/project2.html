Custom Builds

Technology has always been one of the biggest things in my life. Especially the physical aspect of it, like building custom PCs. The best part about building them is that you choose the parts, do the research on the parts you decide to choose, and can decide what you really want to go for.

Custom PCs are 9/10 always going to be better than buying a pre-built. You choose the type of performance you want, you learn how the hardware works together, find out what's compatible, and a major difference between pre-builts and customs is that a lot of the time, pre-builts can have wrong PSUs, as to where you can ensure you choose the right one when you build your own.

Overall reasons as to why you build your own PC

  • Better set performance
  • You pick what parts you want
  • Simpler upgrade by knowing the layout
  • You gain knowledge on hardware and compatibility
